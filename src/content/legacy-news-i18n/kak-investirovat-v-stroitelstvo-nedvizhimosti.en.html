Buying property in Turkey at the construction stage is one of the most promising and profitable investment options for those who want to preserve and grow their savings with minimal risk.

Foreign investors in Turkey are attracted by a simplified transaction procedure, strong demand for housing and fast growth in the price per square metre. In this article, we explain how to choose a property and a developer correctly and how to minimise risks when closing the deal.

How to choose a property for investment

Recommendations that will help you choose a property:

  • Study the market. You need to understand the legal basis of investing in property under construction in Turkey and the specifics of buying and selling. You can study the legislation yourself or turn to a reliable agent. It is also useful to learn the features of different types of property in Turkey and their profitability, and to consider the factors that may affect property values in the future.
  • Plan your budget and strategy. When planning your budget, take into account not only the price of the property but also additional costs (taxes, fees). It is best to have a working strategy from the start — for example, reselling the apartment or renting it out.
  • Choose the right city and area. Turkey has promising tourist regions where apartments will definitely be in demand. But there are big differences between them: quieter areas close to the coast, and lively areas with nightlife (clubs, bars, restaurants). It is most profitable to invest in resort cities such as Alanya or Antalya. Always look at the area's infrastructure: shops, hospitals, restaurants — everything needed for comfortable living.
  • Choose a property in a new development. Pay attention to the complex's amenities, the size, the layout and the developer's reputation. Layouts in Turkish new builds vary from studios to multi-room and duplex apartments.

You can consider both residential and commercial property. It is most profitable to invest at the foundation stage, when developers offer the lowest prices to attract the first buyers. Prices rise gradually as construction progresses.

Choosing a developer

Choosing a reliable developer in Turkey is one of the most important steps. Here is what to look for:

  • Experience. Ideally, the company has been on the market for more than 5 years, which means it has built and completed several projects.
  • Reputation. This is the main indicator of a company's performance. Look at the timing and quality of construction: a reliable developer completes projects on time and to a high standard. You can gather information on specialised forums and read other investors' reviews, but it is better to work with an agent who checks developers professionally and works only with reliable companies.
  • Website. Study the information on the developer's official website: the company's history, news about projects under construction, how many complexes have been built and how fast construction is going.
  • Documents. First of all, carry out a legal check of the developer — it is easiest to ask a lawyer or an agent to gather the information and assess the risks. The developer must have:
    • a licence for construction activity;
    • documents for the land plot;
    • a certificate of state registration;
    • the design and cost documentation for the complex under construction.
  • Visiting the office and the construction site. If you are in another country, you can arrange a video tour.
  • Studying the contract. A contract between the developer and the investor is always signed. Make sure it clearly states the terms and completion date of the complex, the developer's liability for failing to meet its obligations and the procedure for refunds.

At what construction stage to buy

The point of investing in property under construction is to profit from the difference between the initial investment and the final price, which rises with each stage of construction.

There are several options:

  • invest at the foundation stage, when the developer has just opened sales;
  • buy at an early stage and resell under an assignment agreement a few months before completion;
  • buy an apartment six months before completion and then rent it out or resell it at the best possible price.

Advantages of investing in new builds in Turkey

  • High return on investment. Property prices in Turkey rise every year. If the complex is in a good area with developed infrastructure, demand will keep growing, which allows you to earn well on resale.
  • A huge choice of properties. In complexes under construction, you can buy apartments for any budget — not only apartments of different layouts and sizes but also villas within the complex.
  • Attractive prices. Compared with other resort cities in Europe, prices of Turkish new builds are much lower.
  • A simplified purchase procedure. The investment climate is adapted for foreign buyers. You can buy an apartment even from abroad, in just a few days.
  • The possibility of a residence permit and citizenship through investment, with an accelerated procedure.
  • A favourable economic environment. Property taxes in Turkey are low compared with other countries: for example, the title registration fee is 4% of the property value.
  • Developed infrastructure. Social and utility services are developing actively, and many new roads are being built.

How to minimise risks

Investing in Turkish real estate can be highly profitable if you know the possible risks:

  • Forged documents. Make sure all documents are in order. Work with a company that specialises in real estate investment and takes on all the checks — then you can be sure your investment is safe.
  • Unexpected costs. Besides the price per square metre, you will need money for taxes and paperwork. The purchase is not the end of the expenses.
  • A contract in Turkish. All deals are executed in Turkish, which can be difficult if the investor does not speak the language. The solution, again, is a company specialising in real estate investment.
  • Problems with the developer. Possible risks include delayed completion and poor construction quality. Check the developer's reputation in advance and request the full set of documents.

In all these situations, you can protect yourself by working with a company that specialises in real estate investment. It will handle document checks, analyse the developer's reputation, help you find a suitable property and complete the paperwork.

What return to expect

By investing in new builds at the foundation stage, you benefit from the low price per square metre. As the complex is built, the value of the apartments rises: buying at the foundation stage can bring a gain of up to 30%, depending on the type of property. After the complex is completed, apartment prices rise sharply.

An investor who has bought an apartment in a new build can later resell it at a higher price or rent it out.

Rental income

Many people buy apartments in Turkey to let them on a long-term or short-term basis and earn a steady income. If you buy in a region with high tourist potential, you can count on a quick return on investment.

Resale income

This strategy gives a one-off profit. If you invested in an apartment at the foundation stage, you can sell it after completion and earn up to 30% of the initial amount.

The most popular regions among investors are Alanya, Bodrum, Kemer and Antalya. Proximity to the sea and well-developed infrastructure, both in the area and within the complex, are important.
